\subsection{Tissue and Allele Heterogeneity}

Performance varies across the 77 Human and 11 Mouse benchmark tasks. For tuned human TissuePMHC, the ten lowest task means have AUROC 0.6608, compared with 0.8136 over all tasks; individual tasks span 0.6077--0.9640. Mouse task means span 0.5016--0.9180. Figure~\ref{fig:tissue-colored-heterogeneity} shows every task and its corresponding tissue mean. The within-tissue spread is often comparable with the differences between tissue means, showing that tissue identity does not determine performance independently of MHC restriction and data inventory.
