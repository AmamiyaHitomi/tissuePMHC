The fixed test holds out complete pairs within each represented tissue--MHC task but permits peptide or parent-protein recurrence across tasks (Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:fixed-overlap}). It therefore evaluates new task-specific rows in familiar contexts, not entity-disjoint performance or generalization to unseen contexts, independent studies, or prospective cohorts.
